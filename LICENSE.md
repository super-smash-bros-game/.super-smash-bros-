## Core Gameplay


The fundamental gameplay loop revolves around the "percentage" mechanic. As players take damage, their percentage increases, making them easier to knock back. Mastering the art of recovery, edge-guarding, and stage control is essential for victory. Whether you are playing in a 1v1 competitive setting or an 8-player free-for-all, the game demands quick reflexes and strategic positioning.

## Gameplay Deep Dive: The Spirit System


The Spirit System adds a layer of strategic depth rarely seen in fighting games. By equipping "Spirits," players can augment their fighter's stats or gain special abilities like increased speed, weapon resistance, or auto-healing. This system allows for endless experimentation, enabling players to build "loadouts" that counter specific challenges or enhance their preferred playstyle.

## Tips and Strategy


*   **Master Recovery:** Every character has a unique "Up-Special" move. Learn yours to ensure you can always make it back to the stage after being launched.
*   **Shield Management:** Don't hold your shield too long, or it will break, leaving you vulnerable to a massive punish.
*   **Directional Influence (DI):** Learn to tilt your control stick when being launched to survive at higher percentages.
*   **Practice Short-Hops:** Mastering the short-hop aerial attack is the foundation of most high-level combos.


## Troubleshooting


### Game Not Launching
**Possible solution:** Ensure your system firmware is updated to the latest version and that your storage medium is formatted correctly for your console.


### Performance Lag
**Possible solution:** If playing in handheld mode, ensure your power settings are optimized. In docked mode, check that your console is well-ventilated.


### Controller Input Issues
**Possible solution:** Recalibrate your Joy-Cons or Pro Controller in the system settings to ensure there is no stick drift affecting your movement.
